Portaxi

A taxi service for WoW Forever. Sit in an inn, pick a city, and a mage on the network teleports to you with a Scroll of Liminal Passage and opens a portal. Warlocks sell summons. Riders and taxis set their own prices, so rides cost more when taxis are scarce and less when there are plenty. The whole dispatch runs inside the players' addons.

Order

Addon network traffic (hidden channel)
  • Pick a faction and a character in the mock controls. The addon reads faction, race, class, level, learned portal spells, reagents, scrolls, resting and gold on its own: warriors only get the Ride tab, mages and warlocks also get Drive, and each faction sees only its own three cities and its own taxis.
  • Try Quillra (knows only Portal: Stormwind) or Vexira (no Rune of Portals): the addon offers only what they can actually deliver. Gruzhak carries 3g, so a hail with a 3g 50s maximum is blocked.
  • Order a ride as Brennwick, then switch to Velvetmoon. The request shows up on his board with a raid warning, and you can take it from the driver's side.
  • Switch the Market between few and many taxis and watch the prices and the market gauge move.
  • As Velvetmoon, untick Resting or the scroll: Come-to-you disappears from his offers at once, for him and for every rider.
  • Try the unfair case: hail as Brennwick, let Velvetmoon use the scroll, then cancel. Brennwick ends up owing half the price, gets locked to pickups, and taxis see the debt on his next request.

Who can drive

Nobody picks a role, a faction or a price list. The addon reads the character and decides what it can sell right now, and what it can afford as a rider. Anyone who can't drive sees only the Ride tab: their own faction's cities, the taxis and their prices.

Can the taxi deliver?

A mage only appears on the board for cities whose portal spell they know, and only while they have at least one Rune of Portals. Come-to-you also needs the scroll and resting. A warlock needs the ritual, two helpers and a Soul Shard. The portal button itself stays grey without a rune.

Can the rider pay?

Before a hail, a posted price or a booking leaves the rider's addon, it checks GetMoney. A rider with 50s can't post 3g. If the gold is gone by the time the trade opens, the Trade button stays grey and the portal stays locked. A modified addon could skip this check, but the trade would still fail and no portal is cast without payment.

Three services

Come-to-you portal

rider resting in an inn or city + 1 mage

The main service. The mage invites the rider to the party, uses Scroll of Liminal Passage, appears next to them and opens a portal. The rider doesn't move.

Both must be resting. The addon checks both sides before the ride can be booked.

Portal pickup

rider + 1 mage

No scroll needed. The rider walks or flies to the mage, pays in the trade window and gets the portal.

Usually cheaper. The fallback when the scrolls run out.

Summon

rider anywhere + warlock + 2 helpers

The warlock summons the rider to wherever the warlock stands: a capital, a dungeon entrance, a raid, anywhere. With Summon pickup (anywhere) on, riders see each warlock's location and pick the place they want.

Payment happens after arrival, because the rider can't trade before the summon.

Scroll of Liminal Passage: what we know

According to ForeverDB, it teleports you to a party member, and both of you must be resting at an inn or in a city. A Blizzard forum thread describes it as reaching "any inn". I found no official description from Blizzard. Still unknown: where the scroll comes from, whether it's consumed, its cooldown, and whether only mages can use it. Scarce scrolls mean Come-to-you will trade at a higher price, and the market handles that on its own.

Market pricing

There is no price list. Taxis post what they want, riders post what they'll pay, and the addon matches them. When mages are scarce, riders have to pay more to get picked up. When there are plenty, taxis undercut each other.

Four ways to ride

  • Hail: just the fastest. Set only a maximum. The taxi that can get you there soonest wins, and you pay its own price. If two taxis are within 15 seconds of each other, the one that has waited longer for work gets it; you won't notice the difference.
  • Name a price: pay more, go first. You pay exactly what you name. Taxis see requests sorted by price, highest first, so when taxis are scarce, a higher price gets served before a lower one. The addon tells you your place in line and what it takes to be first.
  • Browse. Every taxi that can serve your city, with service, arrival time, rating and price. Book the one you like.
  • Summon to a warlock. A list of warlocks on duty and where each one stands: a city, a dungeon entrance, anywhere. Pick by place and get summoned there.

How work is shared: the taxi rank

  • Own price per service. A mage can ask 2g 80s for Come-to-you and 1g 50s for a pickup.
  • Rank points = minutes waiting since the last ride, +3 for Trusted taxis, −10 for 30 minutes after a cancel. Finishing a ride puts the taxi at the back.
  • When several taxis can take the same ride (a posted price several of them accept, or a hail tie), the one with the most rank points gets it. That spreads the work, so a new mage still gets rides next to a busy veteran.
  • Auto-take grabs the best-paying request at or above your price. Taxis can also pick by hand, even below their own price.

Guard rails: lowest price 10s, requests expire after 2 minutes, one open request per rider, and riders who cancel often get pushed down the board.

Fair play

There is no escrow in the game, so somebody always carries a little risk. Portaxi keeps that risk small and puts it on whoever breaks the deal. The portal is never cast before the gold arrives, so "take the portal and run" can't happen. What's left is a rider who cancels after the mage has spent a scroll.

If the rider leaves the inn before the scroll, the mage's scroll button goes grey and the mage can cancel without a penalty. Summons are the one ride paid after arrival, which is why warlocks can skip riders who owe gold.

Penalties for riders

  • Late cancel (after the scroll or ritual started): marked on the network for 7 days, and the fee is owed to that taxi.
  • Owes gold: until it's paid, only pickups (pay first). Come-to-you and summons are locked, taxis see "owes 1g 40s" on every request, and most auto-takes skip it.
  • Paying clears it: in person or by mail. Only the creditor's addon can clear the debt, after it sees the gold arrive.
  • Three unpaid fees: marked unreliable. Most taxis won't take you at all.

Bonuses for taxis

  • Reliability = share of taken rides that were completed. Shown on every offer.
  • Trusted badge at 25+ rides and 97%+ reliability: +3 points in the taxi rank, so ties go its way more often.
  • Fair share: the longest-waiting taxi wins ties, so nobody gets starved of rides by faster or older taxis.
  • Cancelling costs: a taxi that cancels after accepting loses reliability and 10 rank points for 30 minutes.

Limits: without a server, debts and reliability travel by gossip between addons. A penalty only counts when both addons logged the match, and ratings from your guild and friends weigh more, but a modified addon can still lie. Each taxi decides for itself whom to skip.

How it works inside

A network without a server

Addons can't reach the internet, so there is no central server. Everyone with Portaxi silently joins a hidden chat channel (for example PortaxiNet), and the addons exchange invisible addon messages there. The order board lives on every player's machine at once.

  • HELLO every taxi on duty, once a minute: class, level, place, services it can offer right now, its price for each, rating.
  • ASK a taxi changed a price.
  • HAIL a rider hails with a maximum. BID a rider posts an exact price. BOOK a rider books one taxi from the list.
  • TAKE a taxi takes a request. The rider's addon confirms the first valid one and tells everyone else it's gone.
  • The rest of the ride goes privately over WHISPER.

Addon messages are rate limited, so heartbeats go once a minute and price changes are batched. Channels are split by faction, so Alliance and Horde each have their own market.

Payments and trust

  • Pay in the trade window before the portal. The taxi's addon reads the amount and only lights up the portal button when it's right.
  • Summons are paid after arrival. That's the one ride where the taxi carries the risk, so warlocks can require a minimum rating.
  • A portal lasts a minute. If the rider misses it, the addon offers the mage a free second one to avoid disputes.
  • 1–5 star ratings after every ride, counted only where the addon recorded a real trade.
  • Ratings spread over the network, but without a server they can't be fully secured: a modified addon can send fake ones. Ratings from your guild and friends list weigh more.
  • Local blacklist for riders who didn't pay and taxis who took the gold and didn't port.
